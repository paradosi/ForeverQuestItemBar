# Changelog

## v1.2.1
- Slightly larger small text in the settings panel, and the notes under the settings wrap instead of running past their column

## v1.2.0
- New look for the settings panel, in the same style as the other Forever addons: navy panel, gold accents, two columns. Every setting is still there, plus buttons to bring back hidden items and to move the bar back to the middle
- The drag handle shown while the bar is unlocked has the same look, with a gold light running round its edge
- The GitHub link opens in a matching window, selected and ready to copy

## v1.1.1
- The settings panel shows the installed version and a button with the GitHub link for help and bug reports; `/fqib help` prints both too

## v1.1.0
- New setting *Grow to the left*: the bar's right edge stays in place and new buttons are added to the left, for a bar on the right side of the screen. Turning it on or off leaves the bar where it is
- Fixed an "action blocked" error when combat started while you were dragging the bar: the drag now ends where the bar is as combat begins

## v1.0.0
- First release, for WoW Forever
- A bar of buttons for the usable quest items in your bags, styled like a standard action bar: cooldown swipe, item count, keybind and range indicator
- The bar appears when you pick up a quest item and disappears when you have none
- Bind keys to the buttons under Key Bindings > AddOns > Forever Quest Item Bar
- Shift-click a button to link the item in chat; Alt-right-click to hide that item from the bar
- Settings under Options > AddOns: lock, usable items only, keybinds, tooltips, buttons per row, scale and spacing
- `/fqib` opens the settings; `/fqib lock`, `unlock`, `reset` and `unhide` do the rest
- Its own icon in the AddOns list
