# Changelog

## v1.0.0
- First release, for WoW Forever
- A bar of buttons for the usable quest items in your bags, styled like a standard action bar: cooldown swipe, item count, keybind and range indicator
- The bar appears when you pick up a quest item and disappears when you have none
- Bind keys to the buttons under Key Bindings > AddOns > Forever Quest Item Bar
- Shift-click a button to link the item in chat; Alt-right-click to hide that item from the bar
- Settings under Options > AddOns: lock, usable items only, keybinds, tooltips, buttons per row, scale and spacing
- `/fqib` opens the settings; `/fqib lock`, `unlock`, `reset` and `unhide` do the rest
- Its own icon in the AddOns list
